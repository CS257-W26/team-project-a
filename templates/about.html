About This Project

Global Water Sources v Spending

This project explores how countries use and obtain water resources by combining global datasets on water sources and water consumption. The goal is to support sustainable consumption and clean water access through clear, interactive comparisons.

What You Can Do

Water Usage Breakdown: See agricultural, industrial, and household water proportions.

Usage Over Time: Compare a country's total water use across two years.

Data Sources

The data comes from the FAO AQUASTAT Dissemination System and the Global Water Consumption Dataset (2000-2024). Together they provide country-level context on water availability and use.
